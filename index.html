Meet the Founder

hi, I'm comfort, and I believe that

I spent years as a brand strategist and designer, working with founders on how their brands looked and felt, that is the Brand Identity.

Overtime, I noticed that some founders I worked with were impatient. They had a product they believed in and they wanted to move. So they'd rush through the brand foundation, skip the market research, skip the positioning work, and launched fast.

Six months later, they're exhausted. A year later, they've gone quiet and years after, they are completely out of the market.
